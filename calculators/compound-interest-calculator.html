About Compound Interest Calculator

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal as well as the accumulated interest from previous periods — helping your money grow faster than simple interest.

Use this calculator to see the impact of different compounding frequencies (yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, or monthly) on your final amount.

Formula Used

A = P(1 + r/n)^(n×t), where P is principal, r is annual rate, n is compounding frequency, and t is time in years.

Compounding Effect

More frequent compounding (e.g. monthly vs yearly) results in a slightly higher effective return.
