About Lumpsum Calculator

A lumpsum investment is a one-time investment of a large amount, commonly used for mutual funds, stocks, or other market-linked instruments.

Unlike SIP, the entire amount is invested upfront and grows through compounding at the expected annual rate of return over your chosen tenure.

Best Suited For

Investors with a large sum available upfront, such as a bonus or maturity payout, looking for long-term growth.

Risk Note

Returns from mutual funds and equities are market-linked and not guaranteed, unlike bank deposits.
