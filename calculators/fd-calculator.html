About FD Calculator

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a safe investment where you deposit a lump sum with a bank for a fixed tenure and earn guaranteed interest, compounded at regular intervals.

FDs are ideal for risk-averse investors who want predictable, stable returns without market-linked risk.

Typical Interest Rate

Bank FDs in India typically offer 6.5% to 7.5% p.a., with senior citizens often getting an extra 0.5%.

Compounding

Most banks compound FD interest quarterly, which slightly boosts your effective returns over the yearly rate.
