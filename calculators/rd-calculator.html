About RD Calculator

A Recurring Deposit (RD) lets you invest a fixed amount every month and earn compound interest, similar to an FD but built through regular monthly savings.

RDs are a great way to build a savings habit while earning bank-guaranteed returns on a fixed schedule.

Typical Interest Rate

RD interest rates are usually similar to FD rates, ranging from 6% to 7.5% p.a.

Typical Tenure

RDs generally range from 6 months to 10 years.
