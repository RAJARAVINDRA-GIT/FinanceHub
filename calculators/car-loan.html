About Car Loan EMI Calculator

A car loan lets you finance your vehicle purchase and repay it in fixed monthly installments over a chosen tenure.

This calculator helps you compare offers from different banks and estimate the total cost of owning your new car on credit.

Typical Interest Rate

Car loans typically range from 8.5% to 12% p.a. for new vehicles.

Typical Tenure

Most car loans run between 3 and 7 years.
