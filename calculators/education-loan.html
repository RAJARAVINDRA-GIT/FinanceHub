About Education Loan EMI Calculator

Education loans help students finance tuition fees, books, accommodation, and other academic expenses in India or abroad.

Most education loans offer a moratorium period during your course; use this calculator to estimate the EMI once repayment begins.

Typical Interest Rate

Education loans generally range from 8.5% to 12.5% p.a.

Typical Tenure

Repayment tenures usually range from 5 to 15 years after the moratorium.
