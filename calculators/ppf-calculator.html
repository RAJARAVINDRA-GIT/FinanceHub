About PPF Calculator

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a long-term, government-backed savings scheme offering guaranteed, tax-free returns, with a minimum lock-in of 15 years.

Contributions are capped at ₹1,50,000 per financial year, and the interest rate is set by the government every quarter.

Annual Limit

You can invest a minimum of ₹500 and a maximum of ₹1,50,000 per financial year.

Lock-in Period

PPF has a mandatory lock-in of 15 years, extendable in blocks of 5 years thereafter.
