About Personal Loan EMI Calculator

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for any purpose — weddings, travel, medical expenses, or debt consolidation.

Since personal loans carry higher interest rates than secured loans, use this calculator to find an EMI that comfortably fits your budget.

Typical Interest Rate

Personal loans usually range from 10.5% to 24% p.a. based on your credit score.

Typical Tenure

Personal loan tenures typically range from 1 to 7 years.
